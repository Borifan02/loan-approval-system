# Model Scope, Operational Boundaries, and Limitations

## 1. Dataset Scope & Geographic Context
- **Sample Size:** The Random Forest classifier was trained on 614 records from the Kaggle Loan Prediction Dataset.
- **Domain Limitations:** The dataset reflects retail banking credit practices from a specific historical sample. It does not reflect corporate lending, microfinance, commercial real estate, or revolving credit lines.
- **Economic Shifts:** Macroeconomic conditions (e.g. inflation, central bank benchmark rate changes, employment shifts) are not dynamically modeled.

---

## 2. Model Behavioral Characteristics

### Heavy Sensitivity to Credit History
- In empirical training, `Credit_History` accounts for **55.85%** of Gini feature importance.
- **Practical Consequence:** An applicant with high income (e.g., $50,000/month) but `Credit_History = 0` will almost certainly be predicted as **Rejected**. Conversely, an applicant with modest income but `Credit_History = 1` may be approved if debt service is low.
- **Risk:** New-to-credit applicants ("thin-file" borrowers) who have not had the opportunity to establish credit history may face high rejection rates in this model.

### Simplified EMI & Interest Approximation
- In accordance with the original dataset schema, monthly installment is computed as:
  $$\text{EMI} = \frac{\text{LoanAmount}}{\text{Loan\_Amount\_Term}} \times 1000$$
- This formula assumes an interest-free or flat nominal installment schedule. It does not account for variable APR, compound interest, origination fees, mortgage insurance, or tax escrows.

## 3. Disclaimers & Non-Advisory Status
1. **Decision Support Only:** This application is strictly an analytical decision-support tool. It is not an automated underwriter and does not possess authority to grant or deny credit.
2. **No Guarantee of Real-World Approval:** Predictions and confidence estimates reflect statistical likelihood within this specific historical dataset and do not bind any lending institution.
3. **No Financial or Legal Advice:** Nothing generated by this application or AI agent skill constitutes financial counseling, legal guidance, or debt advice.
